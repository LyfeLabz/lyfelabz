# PS1-8 Physical Performance Requirements — Investigation 5

> **Status:** Internal record consolidated 2026-09-27 from human-reviewed Astra read-only audits and project-owner decisions. Sources: Investigation 5; owner physical-investigation policy; [source register](source-register.md). No implementation, classification change, or production certification is authorized.

## Human-ratified requirement

Students must meaningfully participate in physically separating real matter. Existing mixture classification, property identification, method selection, and explanation prepare for this performance; they do not establish it. The required future investigation remains undeveloped. Frozen classification: **D**, physical classroom implementation gap.

The Massachusetts performance is conducting a separation experiment. A teacher-supplied procedure can satisfy that demand; independent procedure design is not required. Do not import PS1-6's planning requirement into PS1-8. [Massachusetts framework, Grade 6 physical science](https://www.doe.mass.edu/frameworks/scitech/2016-04.pdf#page=60).

## Minimum defensible evidence chain

**Actual mixture → relevant physical property → appropriate method → physical separation → observed result → individually attributable interpretation.**

| Element | Legitimate evidence and attribution |
| --- | --- |
| Starting material and method | Teacher may supply both; students understand the relevant context |
| Physical participation | Meaningful student participation in the actual separation; team execution is legitimate |
| Observations/results | Shared observations can be valid evidence of what occurred |
| Individual reasoning | Connect physical property → method → observed result |
| Teacher observation | Can establish participation and interpretation without full digital duplication |
| Retention | Paper, oral reasoning documented by teacher, digital response, or another usable instructional record |

The audit's reviewability suggestions are not state-prescribed data-storage rules. A group result alone does not establish each learner's performance.

## Requirements not to invent

Independent procedure design, multiple techniques, quantitative measurement, percent recovery/purity, and complete collection of every component are not universal explicit requirements. A single appropriate method may suffice. These additions may serve an instructional purpose later but must not become artificial prerequisites for this standard.

## Unexpected or partial results

Partial separation and unexpected results remain legitimate evidence. Distinguish what students actually did and observed from what they expected. A weak result can support a sound property–method–result explanation; completion alone cannot establish that explanation.

## Future design boundary

Future work must establish a safe, feasible authentic classroom task and appropriate interpretation of its results. This record selects no final mixture, equipment sequence, or procedure. Digital support may organize preparation, observations, reasoning, feedback, and reflection; it cannot replace the physical performance.
