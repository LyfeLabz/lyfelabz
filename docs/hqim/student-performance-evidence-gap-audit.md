## Reconciled finding

Grade 6 has broad instruction and meaningful written, digital-investigation, and physical-engineering performances. Mapped content, badges, or selected-response scores do not establish full performance-expectation attainment. Several strong tasks generate more evidence than their submission records preserve.

HQIM-3's initial optionality assumptions were corrected through human reconciliation. Use the [required baseline](hqim-4a-human-decision-reconciliation.md), not resource placement, to determine inclusion. The [frozen roster](hqim-3a-human-decision-reconciliation.md) is **A = 3, B = 6, C = 11, D = 2, E = 0** across 22 standards.

## Method and evidence boundaries

The reviewed audits traced the Massachusetts verb → actual student task → generated evidence → retained record → teacher visibility → interpretation. Repository inspection establishes authored opportunity and technical behavior at a snapshot, not universal classroom enactment, independent authorship, or mastery. The owner's classroom account supplies authoritative Build-a-Boat context; it is not a claim that Astra observed a class.

| Evidence question | What it establishes | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student performance | The action elicited/performed | That the action was recorded |
| Evidence generation | Writing, trials, models, drawings, observations | That the evidence persists |
| Retention | A record survives the session in some medium | That a teacher can access it |
| Teacher visibility | A teacher can inspect the record | Its quality or sufficient context |
| Interpretation | A defensible judgment tied to the task | Every learner's independent practical participation |

## Strong evidence chains

Amplitude is a strong digital investigation: prediction → trials → data/graph → model comparison → reasoning/CER. The submitted record need not retain the full trial history; that is an evidence-retention/visibility issue rather than absence of performance.

Physical Build-a-Boat and Ball Run provide authentic design and revision opportunities. Paper plans, logs, quantitative trials, individual reflections, and teacher observation are legitimate. Ball Run's retention and PDF/email mechanisms are a prototype, not settled Engineering Challenge architecture.

Body Systems already has meaningful individual synthesis across at least three systems. Biological Evolution already has a whale-origin CER. Improve interpretation and evidence access around these existing tasks; do not infer they are absent or commission duplicates.
