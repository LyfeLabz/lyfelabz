# Internal Review Board Report: Repository Evidence Review

LyfeLabz Grade 6-7 Science. Original self-evaluation prepared 2026-09-17;
repository factual correction dated 2026-09-19 against commit
`4f6f02b01eb988696592265223d2ddff28832eb4` and the Phase 1 audit.

The original report organized its judgments under claimed EQuIP gateways and
CURATE STE dimensions. The rubric identities, numbering, gateway structure,
eligibility consequences, and submission process have not been independently
verified against current official sources. The fourteen topics below retain
that review coverage as internal questions, not official rubric ratings.
EdReports, DESE, CURATE, EQuIP, and HQIM procedural claims are deferred to a
separate official-source phase. No web research was performed here.

## Executive Summary

Grade 6 has meaningful instruction mapped to all 22 standards. The coverage
map retains 22 Primary labels, but these do not establish complete PE
performance. Phase 1 found 16 strongly supported instructional classifications,
five requiring qualification, and PS1-6 with substantial instruction but no
student-planned and conducted reaction investigation. Grade 6 warrants further
evidence preparation; eligibility and readiness for submission are not decided.

The Grade 7 map's 21 Primary / three Partial / four Gap counts are correct as
map classifications. PS2-3 data analysis and PS3-4 investigation are substantive
weaknesses inside Primary rows. Ball-run's existing prototypes and data records
may justify stronger ETS1-4/1-7(MA) classifications after curriculum review.
The three planned gap units are not implemented evidence, and their completion
would not automatically establish a new full-performance attainment count.

The SEP/CCC map and Grade 6 three-dimensional summary already exist. The next
need is accurate source-to-claim tracing, not creating those documents again.
Existing writing, teacher guidance, and some explicit SEP labels were
underreported in the original review. Important performance and response-capture
limitations remain. Adding labels cannot substitute for student performance.

## Evidence Base and Reproducibility

Sources include the two coverage maps, the HQIM narrative and proposed sequence,
the current canonical lessons and root activities, assessment payloads, and
inspected runtime code. Concept families and gap-unit specifications supply
organizing/proposed intent, not proof that planned work is implemented.

The 73-file SEP/CCC scope contains 50 lessons, five investigations, four
simulations, seven challenges, and seven games. All 73 are represented exactly
once. Seven extensions, eight system pages, and eight disease pages are outside
that declared map scope. These groups total 96 instructional-prefix files;

Phase 1 read-only verification passed for the 50-unit curriculum manifest and
all 49 configured lesson builds. Those checks establish source/output
consistency, not pedagogical validity or live production behavior. Historical
platform test totals are not fresh certification results from this review.

## 1. Three-Dimensional Learning

**Internal assessment: substantial evidence, with interpretation corrections.**

Amplitude Challenge requires trial data and interpretation. Eclipse activities
let students operate alignment models. Floatia and ball-run require physical
artifacts, testing, and revision. These are substantive opportunities to combine
content, practices, and reasoning concepts.

The map must distinguish using supplied models from developing models, selected
explanations from constructed explanations, and authored feedback from student
reasoning. Layer Detective displays an authored geologic story after a correct
ordering; that story is not written by the student. Earth's Place supplies a
nested zoom model rather than an independent visual-model construction task.
Repeated examples or topical association alone do not establish a CCC.

Conducting Experiments explicitly labels SEP 1/3/4 and Continental Drift labels
SEP-4. The former claim of no lesson SEP labels is false. Supporting pages use
mixed notation. Terminology consistency is a later review item, not a reason to
mechanically add or downgrade labels. Lexical prediction/reveal counts are not
counts of instructional interactions.

## 2. Performance-Expectation Alignment

**Internal assessment: broad instruction, incomplete performance verification.**

The Grade 6 map separates instructional coverage from student performance and
assessment evidence. ESS1-5(MA), LS4-1, LS4-2, ETS1-6(MA), and ETS2-3(MA)
require qualification. PS1-6 requires an implemented investigation, not just a
badge correction. The map contains eight `(MA)` codes, five in engineering.
Exact official requirements and boundaries await separate verification.

Biological Evolution includes anatomical CER but also substantial
natural-selection mechanism content and quiz emphasis. Grade-boundary
compliance cannot be inferred from changed badges. Some lessons lack PE badges
by design, and supporting activities can provide evidence without badges.

Layer Detective, Amplitude Challenge, and Cell Energy's original unprefixed
badge defects are resolved. Gravity Wells (`MS-ESS1-2`) and Floatlandia Fracture
(`ESS1-4`, `ESS2-2`) still need curriculum review of codes and descriptions.
This report does not decide replacement standards.

## 3. Phenomena and Design Problems

**Internal assessment: strong examples; uniform implementation not established.**

Waves, eclipses, matter puzzles, and engineering challenges provide inspectable
entries into reasoning. Interaction depth varies. The gap-unit specs describe
future phenomena and cannot support ratings of current instruction. Vocabulary
sections are accessible before Engage, so an absolute claim that terminology
never precedes observation is not established.

## 4. Sense-Making

**Internal assessment: real opportunities, with response-type qualifications.**

Students make predictions, interpret models, apply ideas, and write synthesis.
All 50 root lessons contain a textarea. Receiving an explanation or choosing
one can support learning, but does not demonstrate independent explanation
construction. The new distinction should govern citations, not a universal
claim that sense-making is either absent or fully established.

## 5. Evidence-Based Explanations

**Internal assessment: existing tasks need evaluation and retention work.**

Body Systems (`lesson-sources/lesson_body-systems.html`, `bs-thinking`)
requires an explanation tracing at least three systems during a sprint.
Biological Evolution (`lesson-sources/lesson_biological-evolution.html`,
`be-thinking`) already requires a whale-origin claim, anatomical/fossil
evidence, and reasoning. Both have model responses. The original claim that
these written tasks are missing is closed; do not commission duplicates.

LS1-3 needs clearer evidence-based argument/evaluation guidance. LS4-2 needs
consistent evaluation and retention, with grade-boundary review. Chemical
Reactions provides classification and explanation but does not implement the
PS1-6 plan-and-conduct investigation. That is a substantive performance gap.

## 6. Integrated Assessment

**Internal assessment: selected-response assessment is established; performance
evaluation and capture are uneven.**

The 49 authenticated lesson payloads contain 495 `singleChoice` items: Body
Systems has 15, and the other 48 configured lessons have ten each. Ragebaiting
is outside this 49-lesson build/payload population. Writing and physical
artifacts are additional instructional evidence, not automatically scored PE
attainment.

For the two inspected LS writing tasks, submission requires text and reveals a
model response, but the authenticated `lessonQuiz.finalize` calls send only
selected answers. Legacy public submission code includes a `thinking` field.
Production receipt was not tested. Do not generalize task existence into an
assurance that the platform preserves every student artifact.

Ball-run already has trial tables, controlled revisions, comparisons, and
reports. Floatia requires a scaled drawing, tests, and defense. Review their
rubrics and individual evidence rather than assuming data capture is absent.

Planned gap-unit graph/model/history/written submissions are not currently
implemented. `platform/functions/src/shared/types/assessment.ts` defines only
`singleChoice`. Such artifact submission needs an explicit architecture decision
before the specs can be treated as runtime-compatible build instructions.

## 7. Scaffolding and Accessibility

**Internal assessment: baseline features exist; conformance and instructional
coverage require verification.**

All 50 root lessons contain `a11y-canonical`, `mobile-canonical`,
`prefers-reduced-motion`, skip-link, and vocabulary `aria-expanded` markers.
Their presence supports a baseline implementation claim, not certification of
keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, or other accessibility outcomes. Scaffolding
and multimodal supporting activities exist, but consistent multilingual and
learner-specific guidance is not established across the catalog.

## 8. Teacher Support

**Internal assessment: distributed and uneven, not absent.**

Educator Mode/educator-note markup exists in all 50 root lessons. Forty-nine
have canonical lesson sources and generated delivery outputs; Ragebaiting is
root-only. Floatia's Teacher Playbook contains preparation, materials per team,
five-period pacing, safety, checkpoints, and extension guidance. Blanket claims
that no materials lists, pacing, or extensions exist are withdrawn.

No consolidated curriculum teacher guide was located. The root
`EDUCATOR_MODE_GUIDE.md` describes implementation of the overlay, not a
consolidated curriculum guide. A future guide should extract canonical educator
content and reference separately maintained unit guidance, with source hashes
and drift checks, rather than create a second editable copy of lesson notes.

## 9. Differentiation Guidance

**Internal assessment: incomplete and under active development.**

Some guidance exists, including Floatia's material-budget cap or raised cargo target
for extension; it is not a comprehensive differentiation program. The checkout
still contains `G19_GATE_OPEN = false` and an empty
`app/lessons/variants/manifest.json`. These do not establish live production
configuration. Historical staging statements in platform documentation are not
re-certified here. Final capability disclosures and teacher instructions must
wait for the differentiation workstream's completed evidence. This HQIM pass
does not recommend flipping gates or activating production features.

## 10. Instructional Sequence

**Internal assessment: plausible recommended sequence, not live catalog order.**

The seven-family architecture was inferred from existing materials and later
ratified, as its provenance states. The HQIM nine-unit teaching sequences are
different from the live catalog's subject grouping. Conceptual bridges are
plausible but are not proof of implemented prerequisites or learning gains.

The corrected sequence places ecosystem foundations before impacts and requires
the decision-matrix method before planned Units 5 and 8 use it, even though
Design Tradeoffs is listed in Unit 9. Catalog grade ownership and cross-grade
support are distinguished, including Plate Tectonics, ball-run, and Grade 6
Ragebaiting. Related reconciliation in concept-family/gap-unit specifications
is deferred because those files are outside this correction pass.

## 11. Massachusetts Standards Mapping

**Internal assessment: extensive mapped instruction, qualified performance.**

All 22 Grade 6 standards have instructional support. Grade 7 retains 21 Primary,
three Partial, and four Gap labels pending curriculum review. Its PS2-3 and

## 12. Instructional Design Quality

**Internal assessment: promising evidence, not an independently validated rating.**

Source tasks demonstrate meaningful models, explanations, investigations, and
design work. Consistency of architecture is not equivalent to consistent PE
performance, and a planned specification is not classroom evidence. External
review expectations and claims of superiority require separate verification.

## 13. Assessment Integrity

**Internal assessment: narrow implementation strengths are supported.**

Authenticated selected-response attempts are finalized server-side in
`platform/functions/src/assessments/assessment-attempts-finalize.ts`.
`platform/firebase/firestore.rules` denies client create/update/delete on
attempts. These controls support the application's record-integrity design;
they do not establish that privileged infrastructure can never change a record.

The inspected constructed-response limitation in Section 6 also prevents a
claim that every reviewed piece of student work is preserved in an attempt.
No security audit or production test was performed. Claims of national-exemplar
quality or superiority to other curricula are removed as unsupported comparisons.

## 14. Equity and Access

**Internal assessment: useful baseline, unresolved delivery and access evidence.**

Public lesson outputs provide an anonymous instructional surface; authenticated
assignment delivery is a different mode. Source accessibility features,
scaffolding, and some extensions exist. Their existence is not an accessibility
certification, proof of universal access, or evidence of active multilingual
and differentiated production delivery. Final differentiation/access statements
require the owning workstream and targeted verification.
