## Legend

Grade "6-7" marks the ball-run challenge sequence, which carries a grade
selector and is counted by the coverage maps as Grade 6 capstone support and
Grade 7 ETS1 raw material. "Unbadged" in the mapped-code column means the file carries
no standards code; the alignment shown is the coverage maps' inferred mapping.

## Interpretation and Evidence Limits

The formal name "Developing and Using Models" covers different acts. A supplied
walkthrough supports model use or interpretation, not necessarily independent
model development. Selecting an explanation is not constructing one, and
receiving feedback is not authoring reasoning. Goals and educator suggestions
establish intent, not performance. Interpret rows citing a walkthrough, reveal,
or multiple-choice item at that level unless a constructed task is identified.

All 50 root lessons contain a textarea. Body Systems and Biological Evolution
have required writing and model responses, but their inspected authenticated
finalization calls omit the writing; legacy public code includes `thinking`.
Writing is not automatically scored, and production receipt was not tested.

CCC labels are instructional interpretations, not official PE assignments.
Patterns requires reasoning with patterns, not merely repeated presentation;
Structure and Function requires a defensible connection rather than topic
association alone. Where notes cite only classification or material properties,
the strength of that CCC claim remains for curriculum review. Population-data
provenance also needs review before datasets are called verified observations.

## Observations and Follow-Up

1. **Explicit labels exist, with inconsistent notation.** At least two of the
   50 lessons label SEPs: Conducting Experiments and Continental Drift. Ten
   supporting pages also label SEP/CCC: Layer Detective, Amplitude Challenge,
   Cell Energy, Gray Zone, Population Patterns, Protein Pathway, Beetle Island,
   Eclipse Alignment, Floatlandia Fracture, and Gravity Wells. Labels alone do
   not establish enactment or full PE performance.
2. **Strong enacted evidence is retained.** Amplitude Challenge's trial/range
   checks, ball-run's trial tables and controlled revisions, Floatia's physical
   build/test/defense, and eclipse model manipulation are traceable actions.
   Supplied walkthroughs and feedback are different levels of evidence.
3. **Limitations remain.** Retrieval games and a launch hub are not automatically
   investigations or constructed arguments. Chemical Reactions lacks PS1-6
   investigation performance; Heat Transfer lacks PS3-4 investigation; Electricity
   has a PS2-3 data-analysis weakness. Existing LS1-3/LS4-2 writing should be
   strengthened/evaluated/retained, not duplicated.
4. **Grade boundaries and provenance require review.** Explicit Grade 8
   enrichment files remain excluded from Grade 6/7 attainment. Biological
   Evolution's mechanism emphasis and Population Patterns' dataset sources
   need separate review. Catalog grade and cross-grade support differ.
5. **Badge status:** Layer Detective, Amplitude Challenge, and Cell Energy's
   original unprefixed defects are resolved. Gravity Wells `MS-ESS1-2` and
   Floatlandia Fracture `ESS1-4`/`ESS2-2` remain curriculum-review items; this
   pass chooses no replacement codes.
