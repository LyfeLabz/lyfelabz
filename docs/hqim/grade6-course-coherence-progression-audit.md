# Grade 6 Course Coherence & Progression Audit

> **Status:** Internal consolidation, 2026-09-27. Underlying Astra read-only audit findings were reviewed by the project owner; human decisions are identified separately. Sources: HQIM-4/4A, HQIM-5; owner decisions; [source register](source-register.md). No implementation is authorized and no production capability is certified here.

## Reconciled course-level finding

Meaningful local progressions connect Grade 6 concepts and applications. A consistently enacted course-wide progression toward independent performance and cumulative evidence is not established. HQIM-4's unresolved baseline membership has since been settled by [HQIM-4A](hqim-4a-human-decision-reconciliation.md); those earlier status concerns are historical.

The nine-unit sequence is a plausible teaching proposal. It is neither the live catalog order nor a human-ratified mandatory chronology. Prerequisite relationships are strongest within local strands; interleaving remains legitimate.

Conducting Experiments is available as required preparation. The human-ratified progression is learn experimental components → practice recognizing them → reason from supplied evidence → later plan/conduct authentic investigations → analyze own evidence. The owner's classroom connection is melting ice on four surfaces, not student performance of the lesson's soda/Mentos scenario.

## Progression across dimensions

DCI coherence is strongest in connected cells/systems, fossils/time/drift, waves/signals, and properties/materials/engineering. SEP progression includes explanation, data interpretation, investigation, and design; repeated selected answers or supplied representations do not by themselves establish greater independence. CCCs are reasoning lenses, not official fixed assignments to every Massachusetts PE.

Model use is stronger and more widespread than independent scientific-model development. Engineering drawings and revisions are authentic engineering strengths. Investigation 7 narrows the earlier generic modeling concern to actual standard demands; see the [modeling record](scientific-modeling-progression-audit.md).

## Evidence continuity

Explicit carry-forward exists within Amplitude and Ball Run, and in classroom boat build/reflection cycles. Conceptual continuity across lessons is stronger than artifact continuity. An ordinary lesson response carried into a later task, map-to-map synthesis record, Amplitude dataset reused in later wave lessons, and transfer evidence between the two challenges are not established as consistent required records.

A teacher's use of paper or physical evidence can legitimately provide continuity. Lack of automatic platform aggregation is not proof that classroom continuity is absent.

## Resolved and remaining risks

Resolved by owner decisions: Fossil Hunt optionality, Cell Energy/Protein Pathway distinction, map/disease status, required space simulations, both required engineering challenges, and authentic physical modes for PS1-6/PS1-8.

Remaining: local scaffold fading, content depth versus performance independence, exact task placement, attribution in teams, teacher interpretation, scientific accuracy, two missing physical implementations, and evidence carry-forward. A provisional Ball Run → boat progression can support guided evidence habits before more complex material/budget choices; it is an audit inference, not a sequencing decision.

## Teacher and platform handoff

Future teacher support should identify task purpose, preparation, evidence to inspect, likely reasoning differences, and next steps. Future platform planning should represent required experiences and useful evidence without redefining curriculum membership. No scheduling, schema, assessment, or implementation work is performed here. HQIM-3A classifications remain frozen.
