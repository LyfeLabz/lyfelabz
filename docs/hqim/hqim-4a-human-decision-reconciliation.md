## Decisions that supersede HQIM-4

Fossil Hunt's optionality is resolved; Cell Energy is required and Protein Pathway is extension; all eight maps are required and all disease explorations are extensions; Eclipse Alignment and Gravity Wells are required; both engineering challenges are required; PS1-6/PS1-8 require authentic future physical performances.
