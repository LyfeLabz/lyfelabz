## Ratified role and progression

Conducting Experiments is a **required preparatory Grade 6 lesson**, not the authentic investigation itself.

**Learn experimental components → practice recognizing them → reason from supplied evidence → later plan/conduct authentic investigations → analyze own evidence.**

The owner's authoritative classroom context is a subsequent physical investigation involving **melting ice on four different surfaces**. That context was not established by the earlier repository-only audit and supersedes its uncertainty about a classroom connection. It does not mean the lesson itself conducts that investigation.

## Historical mismatch

The audited CER presumed students had performed and measured a soda/Mentos experiment, while the required lesson established neither that physical performance nor adequate comparative results for the claimed conclusion. The mismatch combined unsupported personal-data provenance, wording, evidence sequencing, and assessment validity. The quiz assessed experimental concepts/applications, not a performed investigation.

This finding did not require every preparatory lesson to contain a lab. It required an honest task premise and sufficient evidence for the requested reasoning.

## Current source qualification

During consolidation, the canonical source and generated delivery pages were externally modified. The inspected source explicitly tells students that a science club supplied the data and that students did not collect them. It provides comparative measurements and asks students to reason from those results.

This is **current source implementation in progress**, not production verification. The inspected authenticated finalizer still omits `writtenResponse`. The separate implementation workstream owns the correction, tests, and deployment.

## Performance boundaries

Recognizing variables is not independently designing an experiment. Assembling a hypothesis from menus is bounded preparation. Interpreting supplied results can be meaningful evidence-based reasoning without becoming student-generated experimental data.

Neither the supplied Mentos CER nor the ice-on-surfaces classroom connection automatically establishes PS1-6 thermal-reaction performance or PS1-8 physical separation. Those standards have separate required future implementations.
