## Finding versus decision

**Audit finding:** HQIM-3 traced actual performance verbs, tasks, generated evidence, retained records, and teacher interpretation across 22 Grade 6 standards. It sometimes treated substantive experiences as optional because of platform representation.

**Human decision:** Physical, digital, and hybrid curriculum all count; assignment architecture does not establish optionality. Applying that decision in HQIM-3A changed PS4-1 from C to B and ETS1-5(MA) from C to A. These are historical approved changes, not changes made by this consolidation.

## Supersession record

- HQIM-3A still described Fossil Hunt as optional. HQIM-4A explicitly made it required. Its former optionality gap is closed as a policy question; its C classification is unchanged. ESS1-4 remains a future reconsideration candidate, not an approved upgrade.
- HQIM-3A left Ball Run's required role unresolved. HQIM-4A settled it as required alongside Build-a-Boat. The owner subsequently clarified its summer-program prototype provenance.
- The historical V2 omission explains several B rationales. Later writing-path implementation qualifies those rationales; source changes alone do not reclassify standards or certify production.
- Later classroom boat evidence can inform future ETS2-3(MA) review without changing C.
- Physical-paper evidence is legitimate. Missing digital capture must not be recast as missing physical performance.

## Remaining boundaries

The two D findings concern missing required authentic physical implementations. Neither a concept quiz nor a digital simulation closes them. Modeling and analysis concerns must be judged against actual Massachusetts verbs. A strong team product does not establish every student's reasoning or tool use.
