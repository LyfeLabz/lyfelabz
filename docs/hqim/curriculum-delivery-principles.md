## Purpose and governing principle

LyfeLabz is a digital instructional platform intended to make classroom science stronger, rather than replace it with an online course.

> **LyfeLabz should digitally support every standard, while the required student performance occurs in the environment best suited to that performance.**

> **Digitize the thinking, evidence, organization, feedback, and reflection. Preserve authentic physical performance when the science or engineering calls for it.**

## Curriculum delivery modes

Digital, physical, and hybrid performances are legitimate. Choose the mode by the actual Massachusetts performance expectation and instructional purpose. A physical performance is not a platform deficiency merely because it occurs outside a browser.

## What counts as evidence

Student performance, evidence generation, evidence retention, teacher visibility, and teacher interpretation are separate things. Completion, correctness, evidence quality, independence, and individual authorship are not interchangeable.

Physical artifacts, paper, oral reasoning, measurements, drawings, and a teacher's direct observation can be legitimate evidence. Full digital duplication is not required. A completed interface or a successful product does not establish every claimed practice.

## Curriculum versus platform representation

Catalog placement, an `extension_` filename, resource disclosure, assignment support, a badge, and a submission route do not decide curriculum status. The owner-ratified curriculum governs. The authentic classroom Build-a-Boat design is authoritative; the Floatia web page is a separate representation. Ball Run's technical mechanisms are experimental prototype behavior.

## Required baseline versus extensions

Use the [HQIM-4A inventory](hqim-4a-human-decision-reconciliation.md). The baseline includes 23 ordinary lessons, 17 existing non-lesson experiences (counting eight maps individually and Ball Run once), and two required future physical investigations. Required future work must not receive implemented-performance credit. Protein Pathway, eight disease explorations, and Moon Tonight remain extensions unless the owner redesignates them.

## Team versus individual evidence

Team evidence does not automatically establish individual performance. Shared construction, measurements, and observations can support individually attributable predictions, interpretations, failure diagnoses, redesign reasoning, comparisons, and reflection. A student name on a team record is insufficient proof of independent authorship or practical participation.

## Physical performance

PS1-6 requires authentic physical experimentation with meaningful student planning and thermal evidence. PS1-8 requires meaningful participation in physically separating real matter; a teacher-supplied procedure is permissible. The [separate requirements records](ps1-6-physical-performance-requirements.md) and [PS1-8 record](ps1-8-physical-performance-requirements.md) govern these distinct demands. This consolidation selects no chemicals, mixture, procedure, or investigation design.

## Teacher support

Support teachers in distinguishing a correct answer, a sound explanation, a meaningful trial, a justified revision, and an attributable performance. Existing notes, model responses, and classroom observation count; distributed support is not absent support. Future guidance should build on existing tasks and canonical educator content. Do not commission a redundant Body Systems task merely because map evidence or digital retention is limited.

## Modeling

> **Modeling performance should match the actual Massachusetts standard and instructional purpose.**

Seeing, reading, interpreting, using, developing, evaluating, and revising a model are distinct performances. ESS1-1a and LS1-2 have genuine development expectations. Other standards can require use without independent creation. Scientific-model revision is not a universal explicit Grade 6 requirement. Engineering representations remain engineering evidence. See the [modeling audit](scientific-modeling-progression-audit.md).

## Evidence retention

> **Retain instructional evidence, not telemetry.**

Preserve meaningful decisions, evidence, interpretations, and revisions in an appropriate medium. Not every click, slider movement, or practice error needs storage. Failed trials and unexpected results are real evidence. Latest thinking and best objective score answer different questions; writing should remain attributable to its attempt. Storage by itself does not establish teacher access or interpretation.
