## Team performance, authorship, and evaluation

Shared quantitative measurements can be legitimate team evidence. Individual predictions, causal interpretations, failure diagnoses, redesign reasons, comparisons, and reflections should remain attributable. Names/account IDs cannot alone establish independent authorship. Individual reasoning also does not by itself prove every student built, handled tools, or tested.

A character threshold establishes text presence; a keyed answer establishes correctness; a design outcome establishes performance under constraints. None alone establishes evidence quality, independence, or sound reasoning. A poorly performing design can accompany excellent engineering analysis, and the highest-performing design need not show the strongest thinking.

## Strong local progression and weak links

Ball Run's Day 2 evidence informs Day 3 revision, Day 4 choice/reliability, and Day 5 account; the prototype does not automatically aggregate those records. Classroom boat cycles link quantitative results and accounting to revised drawings and individual reflection. Amplitude links self-generated simulated trials to model comparison and CER.

Weak links include trial/configuration loss at submission, unrecorded same-page retries, map interactions without a cumulative argument record, and team construction without individual observation. These are distinct from missing physical chemistry performances. Model revision is a possible instructional ambition, not a universal missing Grade 6 requirement.
